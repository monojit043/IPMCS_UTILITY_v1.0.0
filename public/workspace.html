Reports
Downloads open in Excel (CSV) and cover the job + unit you are working in. Line data is read live from the line list / P&IDs at download time.
Bill of Material (MTO)
Every MTO row of the unit with its COSMAS line key, line no., zone, header (Contr.Code, DRG, batch) and revision.
Model Dump Data
Every model-dump row of the unit, same columns as the Bill of Material.
Line Zone Status
Every saved line + zone: in zone plan?, status, MTO / model-dump rows, current line data and P&ID revision, last revision, and what changed in the line data since the last save.
Progress — zone plan vs IPMCS
Zone-wise planned (ePULSE) vs MTO taken (IPMCS), left and not in plan, plus line totals.
Pending — planned, no MTO yet
Line + zones in the ePULSE zone plan that have no MTO rows in IPMCS yet.
Revision History
Every saved revision of every line + zone with the line data it was based on (line no., class, P&ID and line-list revisions).
